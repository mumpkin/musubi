Cards live in your notes, written with card: the question, then the answer. The review comes back to each card when you are about to forget it, as Anki does (with the FSRS algorithm).

Challenge 7

  1. Press Space c: the review shows the cards that are due, new ones first-time included.
  2. Enter shows the answer; rate it with 1 (again), 2 (hard), 3 (good) or 4 (easy). As in Anki, a card you are still learning (rated again or hard, or seen once) comes back in the same review, after the others: the review ends when every card is a day or more ahead.
  3. When nothing is due, Escape goes back to the note.

The number next to the vault name, in the explorer, counts the cards due; the header of a note shows its own. A click on either starts a review. To review one folder, right click it in the explorer: Review the flashcards. “Review the flashcards of this note” in the palette reviews the current note.

Reviewing never changes your notes: the history of your reviews stays in .musubi/reviews/, one file per computer, so syncing never conflicts. The settings choose how many new cards a day a review brings (20 by default).
