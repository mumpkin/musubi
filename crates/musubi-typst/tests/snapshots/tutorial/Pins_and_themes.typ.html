Challenge 5

Make Musubi yours:

  1. Pin this note: right click its row in the explorer (Space e shows or hides it) and choose Pin, or type Space ? and “pin”. It appears under Pinned, on every copy of your vault. The four notes modified last are under Recent, and every note under Notebook.
  2. Open the settings with Space , and try the Ayu Mirage or GitHub Dark theme with the arrows. Keep it, or go back to Follow the system. Autosave is there too.
  3. Split the window with Space w v: two panes, Space w w moves between them, Space w q closes one.

The next step is in the graph of the links: press Space g, find the note named Writing Typst and click it. Space g again leaves the graph.
