Helix basics

The editor works like Helix: you first select text, then say what to do with it. Keys do not type text unless you are in insert mode. Press i now to read this note in the editor, and practise on the lines under each exercise.

Modes

The header of the pane shows the mode:

  • NORMAL: keys move and act on selections.
  • INSERT: keys type text. i inserts before the selection, a after it, o and O open a new line below or above. Escape goes back to normal mode.
  • SELECT: v makes every move extend the selection; v or Escape leaves it.

Moving

  • h, j, k, l: left, down, up, right (the arrows work too).
  • w: select to the start of the next word, e to its end, b back to the start of the previous one.
  • gh and gl: start and end of the line; gs: first character that is not a space.
  • gg: first line, ge: last line, 12G: line 12.
  • gw: two-letter labels appear on the words of the screen; type one to jump there.
  • A number before a move repeats it: 3w, 5j.

Acting on the selection

  • x: select the whole line; again to add the next one.
  • d: delete the selection. c: delete it and type instead (insert mode).
  • y: copy the selection, p and P: paste after or before it.
  • u: undo, U: redo.
  • > and <: indent or unindent the selected lines; in insert mode, Tab at the start of a line indents it.
  • ;: reduce the selection to the cursor.

Searching and selecting

  • / searches forward, ? backward; n and N go to the next and the previous match. Text without capitals ignores case.
  • * searches for the selected text.
  • s selects, inside the selection, every match of what you type: one cursor per match. Edit them all at once, then ; or Escape.
  • C adds a cursor on the next line.

Pairs and surroundings

  • mm: jump to the matching bracket.
  • mi( selects inside the parentheses around the cursor, ma( with them. It works with [, {, " and the other pairs.
  • ms( surrounds the selection with parentheses, md( removes them, mr([ replaces them with brackets.
  • In insert mode, typing (, [, { or a quote before a space or the end of a line also types the closing one.

Saving and leaving

:w saves, :q goes back to reading, :wq (or :x) does both, :q! leaves without saving. A note with unsaved changes shows a dot next to its title.
