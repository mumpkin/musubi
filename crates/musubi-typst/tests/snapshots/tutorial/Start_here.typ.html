This tutorial is a treasure hunt: each note is a step with a small challenge, and solving it shows you the way to the next one. These are normal notes of your vault, in the Welcome folder: change them, break them, delete them once you know your way.

Reading and editing

A note opens in the reading view: the note as it looks, not its source. The header of the pane shows READ.

  • j and k select the next and the previous block, gg and ge go to the top and the end, Ctrl-d and Ctrl-u scroll by half a screen.
  • i, or a double click on a paragraph, edits the note at that place. The header of the pane shows NORMAL.
  • In the editor, :q then Enter goes back to reading, :w saves, :wq does both. Space r switches without typing a command.
